Q1: What types of accounts can I open with your bank?
A: We offer:
- Savings Account
- Business Account

🔹 Savings Account

Q2: What documents are required to open a Savings Account?
A: You’ll need the following:
- Commercial License
- Emirates ID (Front and Back)
- Aadhar Card

Q3: Why is a Commercial License required for a Savings Account?
A: As part of our compliance and verification process, a valid commercial license helps us verify the source of income and ensure regulatory alignment.

Q4: Can I apply for a Savings Account online?
A: Yes, you can apply for a Savings Account online. However, physical document verification may still be required.

🔹 Business Account

Q5: What documents are required to open a Business Account?
A: You’ll need:
- Commercial License
- Emirates ID (Front and Back)
- Trade License

Q6: What is the difference between a Commercial License and a Trade License?
A: 
- A Commercial License certifies the legal registration of your business entity.
- A Trade License specifies the types of business activities you are legally allowed to perform.

Q7: Can I apply for a Business Account online?
A: Yes, you can apply for a Business Account online. 
🔹 Other Questions

Q8: Can I apply online or do I need to visit a branch?
A: You can start your application online. The document verification may require sometime and the progress will be shared on the mail as soon as possible. If the verification is not complete, then a mail will be sent requesting for reuploading the documents. 


Q9: How long does it take to open an account?
A: If all documents are in order, account processing typically takes 1–3 business days.

Q10: What is the format suited for document upload.
A: The document must have the same name as their id name
	Commercial Document: commercial.png (pdf or jpg is also supported)
	EID: eid.png (pdf or jpg is supported)
	Similar for all the documents.

🔹 Policy on Irrelevant Questions

Q11: What happens if I ask something unrelated to banking or account opening?
A: Kindly note that we can only assist with queries related to account opening. For questions outside this scope, a polite message will be generated informing the user that the question is beyond the domain of this service.
